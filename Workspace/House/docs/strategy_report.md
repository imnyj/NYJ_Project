## 4. Proposed Strategies

### Strategy 1: Use Bogeumjari as single before marriage -> Refinance to Newborn Special later
- **Pros:** Can secure a lower interest rate immediately without waiting 2 years. Smooth transition to Newborn Special.
- **Cons:** Must buy the property under a single name before officially registering the marriage. May have limits on maximum loan amounts.

### Strategy 2: Use Regular loans now -> Refinance to Newborn Special later
- **Pros:** Flexible, no restrictions on marriage registration. Can buy jointly.
- **Cons:** Higher initial interest rates (~4.0%) meaning higher monthly payments for the first 2 years.

### Strategy 3: Persuade parents to sell their Officetel to secure more cash
- **Pros:** Increases available cash upfront, drastically reducing loan burden and monthly payments. Allows targeting a higher-priced apartment.
- **Cons:** Parents may be reluctant or lose rental income. Could take time to sell the Officetel.

### Strategy 4: Live temporarily in Eunbi's parent's annex
- **Pros:** No housing costs for the first few years. Allows saving aggressively until the Newborn Special loan is available.
- **Cons:** Less independence. Commute or lifestyle adjustments may be required.
