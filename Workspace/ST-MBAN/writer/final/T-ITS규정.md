## Our Authors’ Guidelines

For an abstract to be effective when displayed in IEEE Xplore as well as through indexing services such as Compendex, INSPEC, Medline, ProQuest, and Web of Science, it must be concisely yet comprehensively reflect the article it describes. In particular, the abstract must be:
* Contained, without abbreviations, footnotes, or references. It should be a microcosm of the full article.
* Between 150-250 words.
* Written as one paragraph and should not contain displayed mathematical equations or tabular material.
* Grammatically correct.


Keywords index terms must be included after the abstract and are requested at the paper submission. A maximum of six keywords are allowed with the following specifications:
* A maximum of two optional free keywords
* A minimum of one and a maximum of two keywords chosen from the following list of methodologies:
 - Modelling and simulation
 - Optimization and control
 - System state estimation
 - Transportation demand modelling and estimation
 - Computer vision
 - Image processing
 - Data-based approaches (learning, deep learning, reinforcement
 - learning)
 - Information Technology
 - Artificial Intelligence
 - Formal methods
 - Data analytics and data science
 - Multi-agent systems
 - Cloud computing
 - Human factors
 - Communications
 - Sensor technology
 - Energy management methods
 - Methods for safety
 - Methods for security and privacy
 - Transportation planning and design
* A minimum of one and a maximum of two keywords chosen from the following list of applications:
 - Road transportation
 - Rail transportation
 - Underground networks
 - Public transportation
 - Maritime transportation
 - Air transportation
 - Pedestrian flows and crowds
 - Micro-mobility and sharing mobility
 - Traffic networks
 - Multimodal transportation networks
 - Freight transportation and logistics
 - Ports and terminals
 - Connected and Autonomous Vehicles
 - Autonomous driving
 - Electric vehicles and electric mobility
 - Vehicular Ad hoc Networks
 - Internet of Things
 - Smart cities
